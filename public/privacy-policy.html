1. Information We Collect and Use

Account and profile information

We may collect profile details you provide, such as name, email address, phone number, address, avatar/photo, and country code, depending on how you use the App.

Booking and service data

We process booking details including selected services, pricing, booking status, timestamps, and communication details required to fulfill and manage service requests.

Payment and payout data

We process transaction-related data such as wallet activity, deposit/withdrawal records, payout status, and provider payout method details (for example account holder name, bank name, account number, transfer reference, and payout status updates).

Storage permission

When you use video/photo editing or collage features, we need access to local media files so you can select and save images or videos. This permission is used only to provide these functions.

Purpose: To help you edit and save video/photo content.

Camera and gallery

To enable camera and media features, the app may request access to your camera and photo/video library. We access these only after your consent. If you do not grant access, some features may not work.

Purpose: To enable media capture and editing features.

Microphone permission

When you record video content, we may request microphone access for audio capture.

Purpose: To enable video recording with audio.

Notifications

We may send in-app and push notifications for operational updates such as booking status, payout status, and important account-related alerts.

Location and map-related data

If location-based features are enabled, we may process location or address-related information to support service radius matching, mapping, and operational functionality.

Third-party analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics or Flurry) to understand feature usage and improve the app. Where possible, this data is processed in aggregated or anonymized form.

Purpose: To improve product quality and optimize features.
