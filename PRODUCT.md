# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Operations admins, finance admins, and support/admin staff manage providers, bookings, documents, payouts, and global platform controls in this admin panel. They use the product in high-frequency task workflows where speed, clarity, and error prevention are critical.

## Product Purpose
Provide a reliable internal control surface for Zemen operations: verify provider documents, manage marketplace entities, monitor transactions, and configure platform behavior. Success means admins can complete sensitive actions quickly with clear state visibility and low risk of mistakes.

## Brand Personality
Practical, trustworthy, and direct. The interface should feel calm under pressure, explicit about system state, and consistent across modules.

## Anti-references
- Decorative dashboard aesthetics that reduce readability in data-heavy workflows.
- Ambiguous controls where action intent is unclear (especially approve/reject flows).
- Low-contrast UI patterns that hide critical controls.

## Design Principles
- Task-first clarity over decoration.
- High-contrast action states for risk-sensitive decisions.
- Consistent component language across admin modules.
- Immediate feedback for actions, loading, and outcomes.
- Dense but readable information hierarchy for operational speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Keep keyboard-focus visibility on all interactive elements.
- Preserve reduced cognitive load through predictable layout and labels.
- Avoid relying on color alone for important status communication.
